Privacy Policy — Humsafar

Last updated: 19 August 2026

This policy applies to both apps in the Humsafar family:
  • Humsafar (user app)
  • Humsafar Listener (listener/team-member app)

Humsafar ek friendly-conversation video call service hai — jab kisi ka koi dost na ho, wo hamari trained team se video call par baat kar sakta hai. Yeh koi therapy, counselling, ya medical service nahi hai.

1. Hum kaunsi jaankari collect karte hain

  • Account jaankari: naam, phone number/email, aur password (encrypted/hashed form me store hota hai, plain text me kabhi nahi)
  • Call metadata: call kab hui, kitni der chali, kis se hui — sirf yeh record hota hai
  • Device permissions: Camera aur Microphone (sirf live call ke dauran, real-time me — audio/video kahin store nahi hoti)
  • Push notification token (Humsafar Listener app me — naye call requests ki alert ke liye, Firebase Cloud Messaging ke through)

2. Hum kya NAHI karte

  • ❌ Koi bhi call record nahi hoti — na audio, na video
  • ❌ Screenshot ya screen-recording app ke andar block hai (dono apps me)
  • ❌ Hum aapki jaankari kisi ko bechte nahi hain

3. Third-party services jo hum use karte hain

  • Agora.io — live video/audio call connect karne ke liye (industry-standard real-time communication SDK)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — Listener app me push notifications ke liye
  • Hamara backend server secure cloud hosting (Render) aur database par chalta hai

4. Aapke permissions

App aapse Camera aur Microphone permission maangta hai — sirf video/audio call ke time use hota hai. Aap permission kabhi bhi apne phone ki Settings se revoke kar sakte hain (lekin phir call feature kaam nahi karega).

5. Data retention aur deletion

Aap apna account delete karwana chahte hain ya apni jaankari ke baare me poochna chahte hain, toh neeche diye gaye email par likhein — hum 30 din ke andar aapki request process karenge.

6. Umar seema (Age restriction)

Yeh service 18 saal ya usse zyada umar ke logon ke liye hai. Hum jaan-boojh kar bachon (13 saal se kam) ki koi jaankari collect nahi karte.

7. Contact

Kisi bhi sawaal ya privacy request ke liye humein email karein: sindianforeign@gmail.com
